## Historical workload coverage

- September 6, 2026: two `p6-b300.48xlarge` nodes on EKS in `ap-northeast-2`, eight GPU ranks per node. The 8 B–2 GiB baseline reached 827.38/829.38 GB/s out-of-place/in-place at 2 GiB; hiding the entire OFI library directory produced Socket selection and 14.94/15.53 GB/s, with every exposed EFA byte delta zero on both hosts. Restoration reached 829.44/827.67 GB/s with positive EFA traffic. Both correctness columns were zero throughout. Moving one plugin copy alone did not remove alternative copies. Removing both network/tuner libraries is not a controlled measurement of transport alone.
- A separate idle `p6-b300.48xlarge` pair reproduced private 32 MiB checkpoint-write exhaustion, writer retries followed by peer collective timeout, and recovery to `next_step=1` with zero mismatches. Fork and spawn both completed; the historical hang did not reproduce. FSx quotas and service outages were not exercised.
- September 9, 2026: two PCS `g7e.12xlarge` nodes, four GPU ranks total, Slurm 25.05.7, driver 595.71.05 and DCGM 4.5.3. The held-requeue, alternate-node and restoration sequence was separately exercised. Those manual administrative trials are not the current participant recovery route.
- September 10, 2026: two physical PCS `g7.48xlarge` nodes in `eu-south-2`, eight RTX PRO 4500 Blackwell GPUs and two EFAs per node. A constrained four-GPU, one-EFA, 96-vCPU, 384-GiB allocation per node was only a `g7.24xlarge` resource stand-in, not that physical instance. These historical launchers and scales are distinct from the current Check 5 mapping. They used native SM120 NCCL images on G7, runtime NCCL 2.30.4, aws-ofi-nccl 1.19.0 and EFA installer 1.48.0. PyTorch 2.9.0+cu130 reports build-time NCCL 2.27.7, which is not the loaded runtime version. No image-tag or private-registry availability is implied by a historical measurement.

## September 16, 2026: device recovery qualification on PCS G7

The development suite added physical G7 profiles, EFA binding/domain checks, physical-device loopback selection, Check 5 launch/result handling and DCGM JSON classification. The pair had two physical `g7.48xlarge` nodes, eight GPUs and two EFAs each, Linux 6.17.0-1020-aws, driver 595.91.07, DCGM 4.6.1, EFA driver 3.0.0g and Slurm 25.05.9.

Checks 0/2/3 passed with expected physical counts; Check 6 exercised each EFA and retained cumulative-statistics warnings. An invalid-selector control failed with client exit 103/server ENODATA and zero exposed counter growth. Level 2 passed its software, memory and PCIe coverage. Level 4 timed out at its configured 9000-second limit; cleanup was observed, but Level 4 coverage remains incomplete.

Adopted idle GPU recovery used basic Slurm reboot, not EC2 reboot or active removal. Three consecutive idle cycles retained the instance and restored the selected GPU identity, runtime and a fresh collective/storage job. Active GPU trials stalled shutdown and required external fallback; they are excluded from participant adoption. EFA driver unbind left PCI functions visible while binding/domain/uverbs coverage failed. A degraded-path collective still completed on the remaining EFA at 28.24 GB/s; that was not full-device qualification. Rebind restored both devices without reboot.

Three historical active-EFA cycles completed only after controller cancellation released the old workload. They did not produce a spontaneous collective timeout or arithmetic failure. Those administrative trials are not the current idle-only participant EFA exercise. Teardown errors remain in the original evidence; an old communicator is not made reusable merely by rebind.

Check 5 uses one MPI process per node, eight GPUs per process, a complete 25-row sweep from 8 B through 128 MiB, explicit correctness in both columns, provider confirmation and physical-device counters. Across six post-recovery allocations, maximum bus bandwidth was 38.18–39.64 GB/s. The 30 GB/s workshop floor is advisory for this configuration, not a universal threshold. A deliberately high floor preserved WARN/MONITOR rather than being overwritten by PASS. The optional 2 GiB comparison uses a different mapping and remains separate.

## September 19, 2026: scoped participant recovery audit

Independent source/evidence review accepted the measured non-sudo participant recovery/replacement and fresh workload flow. Retained source, fixed SSM payloads/service serialization, archives and workload/recovery hashes were checked byte-for-byte. This was separate-worker same-model review, not human approval. Full hardware qualification remains withheld. Distribution approval is a separate packaging decision and does not authorize new faults, infrastructure changes or event access.

The [measured provisioning composition](facilitator/DEVICE-RECOVERY.md#pre-session-provisioning--separate-authorization) uses an independent login coordinator, shared environment-independent early isolation merged with native/custom MIME, per-node fixed `ssm-install`, literal admission Prolog and a root-only whole-node scheduling reservation through qualification. The final target has the reviewed runtime; the protected peer retained older maintenance code with a deliberate DMI suite/verifier overlay. This was not a homogeneous rollout or qualification of the peer as an interchangeable replacement target.

Remaining limits:

- No retained first-hook-held setup-failure/access/job-denial trial. The actual failure was a later-boot installed-service `ExecStartPre` delay/false test. Successful fresh boots and local Bash trap tests do not establish the missing variant. This is an evidence gap, not a reproduced bypass.
- IPv6 is not qualified: saved endpoint flags were enabled, but the subnet lacked an IPv6 CIDR and root had no route. No-route is not firewall-denial proof. Measured root-positive/participant-negative coverage is host/enroot IPv4, not arbitrary Docker-forwarded traffic.
- Physical pre-send crash, lost acknowledgement, failed directory synchronization/power loss and malicious alias/other-table key variants remain local fixture/source coverage. Real changed-address retries and pending reboot do not establish those variants.
- Any additional first-hook or reachable IPv6 trial requires separately scoped operational authorization; this packaging candidate creates no new hardware gate.
